\textbf{Key results}
\begin{itemize}\setlength\itemsep{1pt}
  \item Bide+ predicts Bizkaibus arrivals from open data only: the operator's GPS feed and timetable.
  \item Same bus, same stop, same minute: Bide+ is within two minutes of the actual passage in 64\% of cases, the
        official real time in 36\% and the published timetable in 16\%.
  \item With the bus 6 to 20 minutes away, Bide+ is right about twice as often as the operator's own service.
  \item Bide+ is more accurate at 58 of the 60 stops, at every hour and on every day of the week.
  \item The official real time often announces the bus later than it actually comes, so passengers miss it; Bide+ errors are small and
        centred.
\end{itemize}
